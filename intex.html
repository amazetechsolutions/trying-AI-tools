How Patients Choose a Practice Today

A one-page guide for independent practices

The situation

Patients today are often unsure where to go. They search online, compare reviews, and decide based on who feels clearest and most trustworthy. Excellent doctors get overlooked simply because patients cannot tell practices apart. The good news: a few small, practical changes can make your practice the clear choice.

1. Claim and polish your Google Business Profile

For most patients, your Google listing is your front door. Make sure your hours, phone number, address, specialties, and insurance information are complete and accurate. Add real photos of your office and team, and list the services you want more of.

2. Make it easy to leave a review

Patients trust other patients. After a good visit, send a simple follow-up text or email with a direct link to your review page. Aim for a steady trickle of fresh reviews, and reply to every review in a calm, professional tone without sharing any private health details.

3. Say clearly who you help

A confused patient clicks away. Put one plain sentence on your homepage: who you treat, what you treat, and how to book. Make online booking or a click-to-call button visible without scrolling, especially on mobile.

4. Do not leave earned revenue behind

More patients only helps if you are paid for the care you provide. Review denied and underpaid claims regularly, check that coding reflects the care delivered, and follow up on unpaid balances quickly. Small leaks add up over a year.

What to do this week

Update your Google profile, send one review request, tidy your homepage headline, and pull a list of unpaid claims older than 60 days.
